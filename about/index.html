About Me

Hi, I'm Tearri.

Travelling has been my thing since I was a kid. Not in a casual way, either — I mean the kind of dream where you're spending hours looking at pictures of places you have no real plan of getting to yet, just because you like knowing they're out there. That never really went away, it just kept getting longer lists attached to it.

This blog is me actually doing something with all of that. Every time I go somewhere new, I'm paying attention — what I got right, what I'd skip next time, the place everyone hypes up that wasn't worth the wait, and the one nobody mentioned that turned into my favorite part of the whole trip. Country by country, that's what's going here: the honest version, not the highlight reel.
